# Image SEO and asset audit

## Scope and method

Snapshot: 2026-10-21. Complete inventory of every image in `public`: SVG, PNG, WebP and ICO, including legacy duplicates and unreferenced exports. Five MP4 files are not image files and are excluded from image totals; their five poster images are included. Read AGENTS.md and the installed Next.js image guide before changes.

Raster photographs, posters, logos, map and ornaments were visually inspected in temporary, labelled contact sheets using Sharp (outside the repository). SVG XML shapes/IDs were inspected and all Figma SVGs rendered in a temporary sheet; the media play SVG was inspected directly. SHA-256 identifies byte-identical duplicates. Usage searches include all `src` TS/TSX/CSS, data-driven names, Asset.tsx aliases, inline CSS masks and CSS backgrounds. No patient names or identifiers were transcribed. Relationships such as parent/child are not inferred from appearance.

## Totals and measured result

- **144 images**: 90 SVG; 15 PNG; 38 WebP; 1 ICO. Total stored image bytes: **13,782,091**.
- **88 active URLs** (including the conventional favicon URL), **37 retained compatibility aliases**, **19 unused by current source**. Categories are exclusive; an alias reference in JSX is resolved before its old URL is emitted. Unused means no current source reference, not safe to delete.
- Before this task: 139 images. Added four compatibility-preserving descriptive photo copies and one 188,372-byte WebP of the 2,023,825-byte homecare vehicle PNG, preserving 1774 × 887 and transparency. Raw active-origin resource reduction: **1,835,453 bytes (90.69%)** for that image. Both previous PNG URLs remain intact. Storage increases by **881,612 bytes** (vehicle WebP plus four exact photo copies); no disk-space savings claimed. Next.js already optimizes PNG at runtime, so this is **not** a measured browser-transfer/LCP improvement; generated response bytes and cache behavior require production measurement.
- 21 generic photo alts replaced with observed details; three existing factual photo alts retained. Two video poster alts corrected to the actual visible poster, not a later video scene. No gallery ordering, heroSlides structure, video sources or video captions changed. Factual gallery/hero display-text corrections were subsequently authorized.
- Existing exported generic active names already resolve to descriptive aliases in Asset.tsx. Their old URLs remain available. The vehicle mapping now resolves both `imgImage16.png` and `sagara-homecare-vehicle.png` to `sagara-homecare-vehicle.webp`. No indiscriminate rename of unused exports or recompression of already-small SVG/WebP.

## Backgrounds, semantics and follow-up

- `HeroSection.module.css`: `/images/media/sagara-hero-landscape.webp` is the decorative CSS background of `.copy`; no alt attribute applies. The same URL is preloaded in HeroSection.tsx.
- Navigation.tsx applies the four `nav-*.svg` files as CSS `maskImage`; wrappers are aria-hidden and each link has visible text. Those URLs are active even without a literal full URL per file.
- Decorative icons use empty alt, not keyword-stuffed accessible names. Controls have visible labels / aria-labels. Asset.tsx keeps its decorative default. The homecare vehicle currently has empty alt in its decorative display next to the service text; suggested content is recorded if its presentation becomes informative.
- The needle-free benefit icon visually depicts a syringe; its filename describes placement, not literal icon content. Do not claim the drawing proves a needle-free technique. `cauter.svg` is a sun icon, not a medical-device photograph.
- Follow-up correction: four active photo filenames now describe observed count, clothing or location; old URLs remain byte-identical compatibility aliases. Gallery and hero display titles avoid unverified mother/parent relationships and correct the two-adult count. Hero captions also correct white-versus-red clothing, red-versus-purple clothing and the two-children/three-adults group. Generic “keluarga” filenames are retained where no specific contradictory relationship/count is asserted; visible display text uses “pendamping”. Stable media IDs and hero index ordering remain unchanged.
- Video poster corrections do not verify the entire video. No new clinical outcomes, consent claims or patient identifiers are added. Existing publication approval remains an owner assertion, not independently verified by this audit.
- `favicon.ico` was decoded with Pillow and visually verified as the Sagara mascot/logo at 32 × 32; it is already branded. Keep its stable URL.
- Legacy PNG photographs and WebP media can depict the same scene without being byte-identical. Retained, not counted as compression savings or deleted.
